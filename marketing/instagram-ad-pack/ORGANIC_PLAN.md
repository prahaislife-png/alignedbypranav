# Organic Instagram plan: first 4 weeks

Organic posts make the profile credible before people message. Ads bring strangers, and the grid is what persuades
them. Rhythm: **3 posts a week + Stories most days**. Reels with Pranav on camera are the highest-impact content you
can add.

Then rotate the remaining services (wristwatch, office clock, phone number, drawing) using their feed images, the
drawing quote and Reels 5–7.

**Before week 1:** set up the profile, bio and highlights (README, section 7), and post the launch carousel plus the
signature and logo feed images so the grid is not empty when ads start.

**Stories (most days):** reshare each new post; a slide from a carousel with a question sticker (“Which consultation
would you like to know more about?”); a poll (“Have you ever changed your signature? Yes / No”); behind the scenes
(pen and paper, a desk, Pranav’s hands writing, no client material); and every few days a link sticker to the
website or WhatsApp.

## Reel scripts (Pranav on camera)

Format: vertical, 20–40 seconds, natural light, one take is fine. Put the hook as on-screen text in the first second
and add captions (most people watch muted). End every Reel with the same line:
*“If you’d like me to look at yours, message me on WhatsApp. The link is in the bio.”*

Use only facts Pranav is happy to state publicly. Never show a client’s real signature or a child’s real drawing; use
Pranav’s own writing or a sample made for the video.

**1 · Meet Pranav (30s)**
- Hook text: *“I look at signatures for a living.”*
- Pranav: “I’m Pranav Vibhute, a graphologist and alignment consultant. I work one to one with people, families and
  businesses, looking closely at signatures, handwriting, logos, and everyday choices like a watch or a phone number,
  and talking through what I observe. It’s a conversation, not a test.” + end line.
- B-roll: pen on paper, a desk, a logo sketch.

**2 · What I look at first in a signature (35s)**
- Hook text: *“Before I say anything about a signature, I look at four things.”*
- Pranav writes his own signature, then points: “The letterforms. The direction: does it rise, fall or stay level?
  The size, compared with the handwriting. And the flow: one movement or several.” “What those details may express
  is what we talk about together.” + end line.

**3 · The black-and-white logo test (25s)**
- Hook text: *“Try this with your logo right now.”*
- Shows a sample logo (made for the video) in colour, then in black and white. “If your logo only works in colour,
  that’s worth knowing before it goes on signboards, stamps and invoices. I look at colour, composition, symbols and
  balance.” + end line.

**4 · Three questions before a vision board (30s)**
- Hook text: *“Don’t start a vision board with pictures.”*
- “Start with three questions. What do I want more of this year? What matters most? And what can wait? The clarity
  comes from answering those, not from the board.” + end line.

**5 · Your signature vs your handwriting (25s)**
- Hook text: *“Is your signature bigger than your handwriting?”*
- Writes a line of text and signs below it. “Many people’s signatures look nothing like their everyday writing. The
  difference in size and style is one of the things I notice first.” + end line.

**6 · Where is the clock in your office? (25s)**
- Hook text: *“Look up. Where is the clock in your office?”*
- B-roll of a sample office. “Most office clocks are hung once and forgotten. When I work on an office, I look at
  which clocks are chosen and where they sit in the space.” + end line.

**7 · A gentle way to ask about a drawing (30s)**
- Hook text: *“Instead of ‘what is it?’, try this.”*
- “When a child shows you a drawing, try ‘Tell me about this’, or ‘Which part did you enjoy drawing most?’ It opens a
  conversation. Drawing analysis is reflective, not diagnostic.” + end line.
- Show only drawings made for the video. Never real children or their work.
